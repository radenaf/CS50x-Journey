
To characterize the listener's audio aura, I would query the average energy, valence, and danceability:
Query: SELECT AVG(energy), AVG(valence), AVG(danceability) FROM songs;
This reveals the overall mood profile of their top 100 songs.
For example, if energy and valence are high with moderate danceability, the aura might be "Euphoric yet Groovy."
If valence is low and energy is high, it could represent an "Intense, Angry" mood.


Better Approaches:
Calculate weighted averages using play frequency, so favorite songs have more influence.
Show the distribution (median, quartiles) rather than just the mean to reveal range and diversity.
Group songs by mood (e.g., K-means on energy/valence) to identify distinct playlists/moods within the top 100.
Segment data by month/season to show mood evolution over the year.
Create discrete mood labels (happy, sad, energetic, calm) using thresholds on multiple features rather than treating them as continuous values.
Compare the user's audio profile to average listeners to highlight uniqueness.
